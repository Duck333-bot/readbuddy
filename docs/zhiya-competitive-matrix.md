# ZhiyaAI Competitive Matrix and Parity Roadmap

## Verified public capability snapshot

| Capability | Turbo AI | RevisionDojo | ZhiyaAI foundation status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Upload and turn source materials into notes | Turbo AI publicly describes PDF, YouTube-link, and live-lecture inputs with editable notes.[1] | RevisionDojo publicly presents curriculum-linked study resources rather than a user-material ingestion flow.[2] | PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, and Markdown parse into normalized source units; personal and generated notes persist per material. | **BUILD NOW — delivered foundation** |
| Flashcards and conceptual quizzes | Turbo AI publicly describes automatic flashcards, spaced-repetition progress, topic focus, difficulty selection, and explanations.[1] | RevisionDojo lists Flashcards, a Question Bank, Test Builder/Exam Mode, and predicted/past-paper products.[2] | Persistent source-grounded flashcards and conceptual quizzes with evidence and learner mastery signals. | **BUILD NOW — delivered foundation; BUILD NEXT for richer scheduling and topic controls** |
| Lessons and adaptive practice | Turbo AI’s public student page emphasizes generated study activities; RevisionDojo lists Interactive Lessons.[1][2] | RevisionDojo lists Interactive Lessons.[2] | Resumable explain → example → transparent self-check → adapt lessons ranked by learner mastery state. | **BUILD NOW — delivered first loop; BUILD NEXT for richer multi-session sequencing** |
| Exam and curriculum system | Turbo AI positions a general study workflow.[1] | RevisionDojo publicly lists subject catalogs, question banks, test builder, predicted papers, past papers, and coursework tools.[2] | Source-first personal workspace, not a licensed IB curriculum bank. | **LATER — requires content rights, assessment validity, and curriculum operations** |
| Audio/podcast and real-time collaboration | Turbo AI publicly describes generated audio/podcasts and collaborative notes.[1] | Not assessed in this source review. | Not part of this foundation. | **LATER — only if user research shows repeat demand** |

## Product conclusion

ZhiyaAI should not chase broad feature count. Its defensible path is to make a learner’s own material trustworthy, navigable, grounded, and increasingly personalized across reading and study modes. The immediately delivered loop is therefore **material → grounded concepts → notes/cards/quiz/lesson → transparent learner signals**, while licensed curriculum banks, collaboration, audio, and exam-mode systems remain deferred.
