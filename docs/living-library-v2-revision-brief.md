# Living Library v2 — Revision Brief

## The correction

Living Library v1 established a useful foundation—warm paper, deep ink, a calm reader, and a connection motif—but it spread atmosphere too broadly. It risked making ReadBuddy feel like a polished literary-themed AI product rather than a world-class consumer product. V2 retains the foundation but changes the center of gravity: **product proof first, brand world second.**

The product has one recognizable behavior, not a collection of aesthetic ingredients: the **ReadBuddy Thread**. It appears when ReadBuddy makes a specific, useful connection—current sentence to earlier passage, upload stage to book structure, recent reading to remembered concept. If no connection is being shown, no thread appears.

## Keep / remove / replace

| Keep | Remove | Replace with |
|---|---|---|
| Warm paper + deep ink | Stars and constellations as generic decoration | One precise thread with a page, concept, or memory endpoint |
| Color at meaningful moments | Soft lavender as a page-level mood | High-contrast violet/sky only for intelligence and direct action |
| Full-screen Book Brain moment | Decorative floating-book artwork | A clear visual model of chapters, characters, concepts, and connections forming |
| Library as personal space | Playful dancing cover grid | Gallery-like covers with generous scale and a dominant current journey |
| Quiet reader | AI card look and familiar UI shadows | Annotation-like intelligence with an evidence thread in the margin |

## Rules for every screen

1. The first three seconds must show **what the product does**, not merely its mood.
2. One screen gets one primary compositional idea. A surface does not need a card to be organized.
3. ReadBuddy colors signal a role. They are not confetti.
4. The app must be recognisable without its wordmark because its thread, typography, spacing, and hierarchy are consistent.
5. Illustration is earned by an interaction. It may explain a book becoming known; it may not fill empty space.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid generic feature-card grids, default dialogs, default shadows, rounded rectangles as universal containers, repeated sparkles, full-page gradients, and ornamental cosmic scenes. V2 should read as **modern, editorial, exact, and confident**—not whimsical, mystical, or startup-cute.
