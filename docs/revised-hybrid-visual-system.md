# ReadBuddy Revised Hybrid Visual System

## Decision

ReadBuddy will use a **contemporary, colorful, artistic consumer-product language**. It keeps the Living Book’s intelligence, Intellectual Editorial’s restraint, and Reading Journey’s cinematic visual storytelling, but removes every black-neon, purple-glow, antique-fantasy, academic-austere, and generic AI-SaaS cue.

The governing image is not a magical book, a research tool, or a dashboard. It is a **living field of ideas**: a current sentence releases small, disciplined fragments of context—an earlier passage, a page edge, an annotation mark, and one calm explanation—then returns the reader to the book.

## Source principles, translated rather than copied

The reference products suggest four useful rules. RevisionDojo uses a friendly, visually active system to make a broad learning product feel approachable. Turbo AI uses an upload-to-result story to make its value immediate. Lovable foregrounds a clear product promise with minimal navigation and a direct call to action. ReadBuddy will use only these high-level principles: **one legible product action, controlled visual energy, large atmospheric color fields, and a concrete transformation shown before explained**. It will not copy their layouts, brand marks, component styling, or claims.[1] [2] [3]

## Visual personality

| Trait | ReadBuddy expression | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Warm intelligence | A book passage creates a soft, connected field of annotations | Never portray intelligence as a glowing robot, star map, or chat assistant |
| Contemporary art | Paper fragments, translucent color blocks, loose editorial lines, and cropped page coordinates | Never use castles, antique maps, parchment illustrations, or historical manuscript motifs |
| Consumer desire | Large color fields, tactile depth, generous scale, and a calm visual reveal | Never fill the screen with cards, badges, gradients, or a feature-grid dashboard |
| Trust | Clean citations, quiet privacy language, and a visibly separate “book says” versus “ReadBuddy helps” treatment | Never use an academic journal voice everywhere or bury evidence in decorative graphics |
| Reading first | The reader is soft paper, deep ink, and nearly no ornament | Never carry the cinematic marketing art behind reading paragraphs |

## Palette system

The palette is a set of **controlled pairings**, not a rainbow. Each marketing section uses one atmospheric field, one paper/light anchor, one deep-ink anchor, and one small signal color.

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#FBF7EF` warm off-white | Reader, type contrast, negative space |
| Ink | `#17233D` deep ink blue | Headlines, navigation, reading controls |
| Periwinkle | `#AEB8FF` | Memory connections and large quiet atmospheric fields |
| Powder blue | `#BFE8F5` | Evidence/source moments and cool depth |
| Blush | `#F6B7C7` | Human annotation and selected text response |
| Apricot | `#FFB07A` | Transformation and motion focal points |
| Mint | `#B7E6C5` | Understood/settled context and privacy reassurance |
| Warm yellow | `#F4CF5A` | Rare action and current-reading signal only |

> The rule is **two colored fields plus paper and ink** per major moment. Periwinkle, blush, apricot, mint, and yellow must never compete in one dense view.

## Proprietary ReadBuddy graphic language

The visual system uses five original, repeatable primitives.

| Primitive | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Margin thread** | A present question connects to already-read evidence | One flowing line with a slightly squared end-cap; never constellation dots |
| **Page shard** | A precise remembered piece of the book | A cropped, rounded paper fragment with a page coordinate |
| **Evidence bracket** | The book is the source | Two thin, offset brackets around cited text or a page label |
| **Understanding bloom** | A hard sentence has become clearer | A small, layered color halo made from translucent paper-like shapes, not a glow |
| **Return tab** | The reader can always go back to their place | A compact folded tab with a directional notch |

These primitives become the icon language. They replace generic sparkles, robots, orbital graphics, and decorative stock icons.

## Marketing storyboard: 0% to 100%

| Scroll point | Scene | Product truth shown | Motion rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0%** | A single open reading surface enters a periwinkle/apricot atmospheric field | Difficult reading can feel beautiful, not lonely | Color field eases in; no text movement yet |
| **25%** | A reader highlights one difficult sentence; a margin thread begins | Help starts from the reader’s actual moment | Thread draws once after selection, under 450ms |
| **50%** | One page shard slides into view with an evidence bracket and `p.47` | ReadBuddy remembers earlier pages | Shard moves 16–24px; citation stays still |
| **75%** | The explanation settles beside the passage; warm colors resolve into mint/paper | The reader gets unstuck without leaving the book | Bloom expands once, then becomes a static background shape |
| **100%** | The return tab carries the reader back to their paragraph; the book fills the field again | AI gets out of the way after helping | All decorative layers fade; book remains |

## Reader discipline

The reader uses Direction B’s discipline: warm paper, deep ink, highly legible literary typography, compact evidence notes, and strong separation between the book text and ReadBuddy’s interpretation. The only color in normal reading is a restrained evidence bracket, selected-text wash, and small return tab. The marketing palette does not become reader chrome.

## Non-negotiable exclusions

No neon black interface. No purple AI glow. No constellation field. No fantasy or storybook architecture. No parchment/cartography illustration. No historical-map language. No generic feature-card SaaS homepage. No fabricated user reviews, company logos, usage counts, or institutional claims.
