## Interview — capture answers verbatim

1. What did you think ReadBuddy was for before you clicked anything?
2. What felt different from asking a normal AI chatbot?
3. Which moment, if any, made you want to keep using it?
4. Did the evidence links make you more or less trusting?
5. Did any part feel distracting, unclear, or like extra work?
6. Would you upload your next real book here? Why or why not?
7. What would you miss most if ReadBuddy disappeared tomorrow?
8. What is one thing you would change before using it every week?
