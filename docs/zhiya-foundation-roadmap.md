# ZhiyaAI Competitive Position and Roadmap

## Competitive reading

Turbo AI publicly emphasizes transforming uploaded study materials into study guides, flashcards, quizzes, and explanations, while RevisionDojo emphasizes a curriculum-shaped revision environment with practice and resource libraries. ZhiyaAI’s current differentiation is not a copied interface; it is the shared Material Intelligence plus Reader Intelligence foundation that can preserve source evidence and narrative spoiler boundaries for books while supporting broader learning materials.[1] [2]

| Capability | ZhiyaAI now | Turbo AI public emphasis | RevisionDojo public emphasis | Product decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Upload many learning formats | PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, Markdown | Material upload and transformation | Curated revision resources | **Build now:** complete parser and workspace foundation. |
| Source-grounded study artifacts | Notes, concepts, flashcards, quiz, lesson carry Material evidence | Study-resource generation | Practice/revision content | **Build now:** core trust moat. |
| Narrative book reading with spoiler awareness | Existing Reader and Book Brain preserve this | Not established by reviewed public page | Not established by reviewed public page | **Protect:** unique reading advantage. |
| Learner model | Explicit mastery states from answers and self-assessments | Public progress/study features | Public revision progress | **Build next:** cross-Material recommendations after user evidence. |
| Curriculum catalog | No built-in catalog | Not the primary reviewed focus | Major curriculum/IB emphasis | **Later:** only after validating a target curriculum and rights model. |
| Shared workspaces and collaboration | Not shipped | Not assessed as current priority | School/education positioning | **Later:** do not add before solo learning retention is measured. |

## BUILD NEXT

The highest-value next work is reliability and depth rather than feature breadth: real office-file uploads through the complete UI acceptance flow, source-view jumps from every study artifact, user-visible analysis history, more robust scheduled-job observability, and a five-reader alpha study focused on whether learners return to cards, quizzes, and lessons after reading.

## BUILD LATER

Cross-Material concept relationships, practice recommendations across a learner’s library, optional study reminders, collaboration, curriculum libraries, and tutor chat should wait until the single-Material learning loop demonstrates repeat use. Automated scheduling must be added only with clear user control and cost visibility.

## DON’T BUILD YET

Do not add a generic chat-first tutor, public sharing by default, opaque automatic grading of written answers, an ungrounded “AI study plan,” fabricated social proof, or features that move reading and evidence into the background.
