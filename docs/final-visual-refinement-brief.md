# ReadBuddy Final Visual Refinement Brief

## Purpose

This is the final visual refinement before the limited hero prototype. It does **not** authorize production UI work. The task is to make ReadBuddy 30–40% more vivid and memorable without weakening its existing trust, evidence, typography, or reader restraint.

## Art-direction correction

The previous visual package proved that ReadBuddy can feel tactile, calm, and trustworthy. This refinement increases **color as composition**, rather than applying more accents to the same muted literary system.

The marketing visual world becomes a set of large, deliberate atmospheric pairings. A moment may use `periwinkle + apricot + paper`, `powder blue + blush + paper`, or `mint + periwinkle + paper`, with one saturated **ReadBuddy signal orange** used sparingly for the active connection or resolved insight. Deep ink is a structural color, not a full-screen atmosphere.

## Visible intelligence sequence

The marketing hero must make ReadBuddy’s intelligence unmistakable in one visual sentence:

> **Current sentence → earlier page fragment → page coordinate and evidence → contextual explanation → connection resolved → return to reading.**

This sequence uses one Margin Thread and at most one Page Shard at a time. It is not a network, constellation, technical diagram, or chat interaction. The surprise comes from the precise reorganization of already-read material around the reader’s immediate difficulty.

## Art and composition rules

| Keep | Increase | Remove |
|---|---|---|
| Margin Thread, Page Shard, Evidence Bracket, Return Tab, tactile paper quality, restrained reader | Large paired color fields, saturated signal orange, asymmetric page-fragment compositions, visible evidence sequence, abstract thought/memory forms | Botanical imagery, hands, lifestyle props, fantasy, antiquarian objects, parchment, historical maps, constellations, node networks, generic AI sparkles |

The art must originate from books, type, annotations, page fragments, coordinates, evidence, and abstract memory forms. It should look equally natural beside a novel, economics text, scientific paper, or textbook.

## Reduced proprietary motif system

The previous **Understanding Bloom** is removed. It is attractive but insufficiently tied to reading. The definitive system has four ownable primitives:

| Primitive | Visual function |
|---|---|
| **Margin Thread** | Directs attention from the current sentence to the one earlier source that matters. |
| **Page Shard** | Holds a retrieved earlier passage as a physical, cropped evidence fragment. |
| **Evidence Bracket** | Separates what the book says from ReadBuddy’s contextual help. |
| **Return Tab** | Signals that the reader can return to their place after exploring evidence. |

## Brand-mark exploration criteria

The current reader-head/book mark is only an exploration and will not be treated as final. The new mark study must show three to five simple adult-capable options. Each option must work at favicon scale, feel credible for both students and serious adult readers, and derive from one of the four reading primitives rather than a mascot, smiling face, childish literacy symbol, robot, or generic AI sparkle.

## Reader rule

The reader does not inherit the new marketing color density. It remains warm paper, deep ink, generous literary typography, pale selection wash, compact evidence bracket, and a minimal return tab. Marketing is cinematic; reading is nearly invisible.

## Final deliverables

The founder will review six assets only: revised desktop hero, revised mobile hero, color-in-use board, logo/symbol exploration, reduced motif system, and updated five-stage scroll storyboard. No production code, page change, motion implementation, icon implementation, or visual-system rollout follows without explicit approval.
