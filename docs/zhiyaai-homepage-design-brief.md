# ZhiyaAI homepage design brief

The reference at [turbo.ai](https://www.turbo.ai/) was reviewed only for broad product-marketing structure: a compact header, a clear two-column hero, a direct activation action, and a visible product preview. No Turbo AI copy, brand marks, mascot, illustration, customer logos, metrics, or interface assets will be reused.

ZhiyaAI uses an original reading-specific expression: a lavender-and-blush field, an in-page/evidence product preview, the promise **“Read deeper. Remember more.”**, and the reading-native benefits “Keep your place,” “Find earlier context,” and “Understand without spoilers.” The unique signature is the visual connection between a current passage and a safe earlier passage—not a study-material generator.

The visible app rebrand changes product-facing text, the shared wordmark, public metadata, boot state, account entry, reader helper, upload helper, and future email wording. Internal service identifiers, OAuth redirect URLs, database fields, existing domain, and repository name remain unchanged to preserve production behavior. A real branded custom domain and external Google-consent application name must be handled separately after the product rebrand is verified.
