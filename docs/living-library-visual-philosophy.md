# Living Library

**Living Library** treats reading as navigation through a private night sky. A book is not a rectangular file or a set of pages; it is a landscape with paths, constellations, crossings, and places worth returning to. The identity is built from the quiet tension between luminous paper and deep ink. Its signature is a thread: a fine, energized line that appears only when one thought discovers another. The visual language must feel meticulously crafted, emotionally intelligent, and unmistakably native to reading rather than borrowed from software.

Space is purposeful. Outer product surfaces use cinematic fields, generous asymmetric emptiness, saturated points of light, and book-like objects that have gravity. The landing page opens a world before it explains a feature. The library behaves like an unfolding atlas of intellectual life, allowing covers, recent journeys, and unfinished paths to breathe. Interfaces use fewer containers and stronger compositions; every major screen is the result of painstaking attention to hierarchy, rhythm, and material contrast.

Color communicates role rather than decoration. Paper is luminous and warm. Ink is midnight, confident, and dense. Electric violet marks intelligence, sky marks retrieval, sun marks progress and invitation, coral marks human warmth, and mint marks completion. These colors appear as deliberate signals in a controlled semantic system, never as scattered styling. High saturation is reserved for moments of connection: a path forming, a passage found, a book becoming known.

Typography is the product’s voice. An expressive display serif gives books and brand moments their literary gravity. A calm humanist sans keeps actions and systems clear. A dedicated reading serif gives the reader long-form comfort. Type is sized from a finite scale, with generous leading and deliberate contrast; it is never a collection of arbitrary values. The product should appear masterfully typeset, as if each line had been laboriously positioned by a designer who reads closely.

Motion has four jobs only. **Reveal** lets a scene arrive with measured confidence. **Lift** gives covers and book objects physical presence. **Thread** traces an intelligent connection from a current moment to an earlier one. **Focus** tightens the reader’s attention around a selection, answer, or passage. Every movement is restrained, interruptible, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences. The reader itself remains almost silent: monochrome paper, quiet controls, one small thread in the margin when the book remembers something important.
